## Limits of this test

- The stress scenarios still come from this repository's generator. They change the data in ways the detector was not designed around, but they are not real claims.
- Predictions were registered in advance, so a hit means the failure was understood, not that it is acceptable. Misses are reported as they are; ranges were not widened afterwards.
- Each scenario changes one thing at a time. Real data would combine several.
